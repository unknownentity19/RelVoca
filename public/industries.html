Frequently asked questions

How can businesses across different industries use AI chatbots and voice assistants?

AI chatbots and voice assistants help businesses in various industries streamline operations, improve customer engagement, and automate repetitive tasks. In customer service, AI agents handle inquiries, provide 24/7 support, and reduce response times. In healthcare, they assist with appointment scheduling and patient FAQs, while in e-commerce, they personalize shopping experiences and manage order tracking. Industries like finance, law, and real estate leverage AI assistants for lead qualification, compliance support, and document automation.

‍

With RelVoca, businesses can design AI-powered chat and voice experiences tailored to their industry’s needs. Whether it's an AI receptionist for law firms, a virtual banking assistant, or a hospitality concierge, RelVoca’s flexible platform enables teams to create, test, and deploy AI agents that enhance efficiency and improve customer satisfaction.

Does RelVoca integrate with existing enterprise software and CRMs?

Yes, RelVoca integrates seamlessly with enterprise software, CRMs, and other business tools to create a fully connected AI experience. With API support and webhooks, businesses can sync their AI agents with platforms like Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, and more, ensuring smooth data flow between systems. This allows AI assistants to pull customer details, update records, and trigger workflows based on real-time interactions.
‍

Additionally, RelVoca supports integrations with knowledge bases, ticketing systems, and internal databases, enabling AI agents to provide accurate, context-aware responses. Whether automating lead management, handling support tickets, or streamlining internal processes, RelVoca’s flexible integration options help businesses maximize efficiency while maintaining a cohesive digital ecosystem.

How easy is it to customize an AI agent for specific industry needs using RelVoca?

RelVoca makes it easy for businesses to customize AI agents for their specific industry needs: without requiring extensive coding. The platform offers a visual drag-and-drop interface, allowing teams to design conversational experiences tailored to their workflows, customer interactions, and compliance requirements. Businesses can integrate custom APIs, connect to existing databases, and personalize responses based on user intent, ensuring their AI agent aligns with industry-specific use cases.

‍

Additionally, RelVoca provides pre-built templates for industries like healthcare, finance, legal services, and customer support, enabling quick deployment with minimal setup. With flexible integrations and natural language processing (NLP) capabilities, companies can create AI assistants that adapt to evolving business needs while delivering a seamless user experience.
