Frequently asked questions

How does the agent stay accurate about our product?

Answers are grounded in your product documentation and the customer's own account data, and anything outside that scope escalates instead of being invented.

Can it take real actions on accounts?

Yes — agents act through your existing APIs and billing system with the permissions you define, and every action is logged with the transcript.

What happens on plan or contract decisions?

Agents are scoped to servicing and information. Commercial judgment calls route to your team with the account context and conversation attached.
