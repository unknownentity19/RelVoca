Frequently asked questions

What can the agent resolve on its own?
Any call your policies allow it to complete: it reads live data from your systems, takes the approved action, and confirms the outcome. You decide the boundary; everything outside it goes to a person.
What happens when it cannot resolve something?
It hands off to your team with the transcript and the fix already scoped, so the customer never repeats themselves. Once the person resolves the edge case, the agent takes back over.
Does this replace the tools we already run?
No — the agent plugs into your existing stack, resolves the routine volume inside it, and logs every action there, so your team keeps one system of record.
How do we keep it inside policy?
Guardrails are explicit: you define what it may read, what it may change, and what it must refuse. It declines unsafe shortcuts, explains why, and routes edge cases to human review.
